Overview

A classic bluffing & deduction game for 4+ players. Every player gets one card. One holds the Police card, one holds the Thief (always worth 0). The Police must guess who's holding the Thief — guess right and the Police scores the reward, guess wrong and the Thief scores it instead.

How to Play

1
Everyone receives one hidden card.
2
Whoever holds the Police card becomes the Police for the round — this is a separate card from the highest-value one, and could belong to anyone.
3
The Police card and that round's highest-value card are both revealed to everyone by default. The Thief card is always worth 0 — and stays hidden until the guess.
4
The Police studies the other players.
5
Police chooses a suspect and confirms the accusation.
6
Correct → Police gets the reward. Wrong → the Thief gets it instead.
7
After all rounds, the highest total score wins.
